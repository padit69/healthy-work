# WorkWell Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 19, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 19, 2026

WorkWell is a macOS wellness-reminder application provided by **HiHi Team**. This Privacy Policy explains how WorkWell handles information when you use the app.

## Summary

WorkWell is designed to work locally on your Mac.

- No account is required.
- We do not collect your personal data.
- We do not sell or share personal data.
- We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
- Your settings and wellness activity records remain on your Mac.

## Information stored on your Mac

WorkWell may store the following information locally on your device:

- Reminder preferences and intervals.
- Work and lunch schedules.
- Optional weight and profile settings used to estimate a general daily hydration goal.
- Water entries and reminder-completion history.
- Streak and activity statistics.
- Appearance, language, notification, and Start at Login preferences.

This information is used only to provide WorkWell's features. It is not transmitted to HiHi Team or stored on our servers.

## Notifications

WorkWell requests permission to send macOS notifications for water, eye-rest, and movement reminders. Notification delivery is handled by macOS. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in **System Settings → Notifications → WorkWell**.

## Start at Login

WorkWell offers an optional Start at Login setting. The app registers itself as a login item only after you explicitly enable this option. You can disable it at any time in WorkWell or in macOS System Settings.

## Network access and external links

The Mac App Store version of WorkWell receives application updates through the Mac App Store. WorkWell does not transmit your locally stored wellness information to HiHi Team.

If you choose to open an external website, such as the WorkWell support or source-code page, that website is governed by its own privacy policy. WorkWell does not control how an external website processes information after it opens in your browser.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

WorkWell does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies. We do not create advertising profiles or track your activity across apps or websites.

## Data sharing

Because WorkWell does not collect your personal data, we do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

Your WorkWell data remains locally on your Mac until you remove it through the app, clear the app's local data, or uninstall the app and remove its container data. HiHi Team does not retain a server-side copy and cannot retrieve or delete information stored only on your device.

## Data security

WorkWell relies on macOS application sandboxing and system security protections. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you should keep macOS updated and protect access to your user account.

## Children

WorkWell is a general wellness utility and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Health and medical information

WorkWell provides general wellness reminders only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Hydration estimates and break suggestions are general guidance and may not be appropriate for every person or health condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional when you have questions about your health.

## International users

WorkWell's local data remains on your device unless you independently choose to share it. Because HiHi Team does not collect this data, it is not transferred by WorkWell to HiHi Team across national borders.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WorkWell's features or data practices change. Any revision will be published on this page with an updated effective date. If a change materially affects how the app handles information, we will provide appropriate notice through the app or its App Store listing.
